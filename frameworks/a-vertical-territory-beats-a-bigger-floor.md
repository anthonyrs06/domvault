# A Vertical Territory Beats a Bigger Floor

> A cat does not win the house by owning more carpet. It wins by stacking: the fridge, the sill, the shelf that turns air into a second floor.

## The connection
Felids expand in three dimensions. Floor area is a poor map of who actually lives here. Knowledge graphs make the same mistake: more files, more folders, more carpet. Stacking is the real range. A neuron sits on two frameworks. A spark is a shelf you have not climbed yet. Hoarding markdown is a bigger floor. Linking layers is vertical territory.

## Why it matters
A vault that only grows sideways is a house with no cat tree. The public constellation cannot fly through a pile. It can fly through stacked neighborhoods: galaxy, framework, synthesis, the cheap hallway between them. Rank is still the wrong ontology. Height is not rank. Height is another room.

## So what
- Prefer a new layer on an existing note over a new file on the same floor.
- Measure range by shelves (links, neurons), not by square footage (note count).
- If two ideas share air, stack them. Do not buy another wing of the house.
