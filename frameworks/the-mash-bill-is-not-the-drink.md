# The Mash Bill Is Not the Drink

> Recipe is not the aged pour. A mash bill is a promise about grain. The drink is what the barrel, the rickhouse, and the years actually served.

## The connection
A mash bill is the recipe: corn, rye, malt, the ratios you can write on a card. The drink is the liquid after wood, weather, and evaporation have had a vote. Outline a note and you have mash. Sit it in a graph of other notes and you have a barrel. The same mash in two warehouses is two drinks. A second brain that ships the outline is selling grain as whiskey.

## Why it matters
People confuse the recipe with the pour because the recipe is fast and the barrel is slow. A constellation that indexes mash bills will taste like a pitch deck. Time in the wood still matters. So does the neighborhood the cask sat in. The graph is the warehouse climate.

## So what
- Score a note by the graph it sits in, not by the outline it started as.
- Do not publish the mash bill and call it a tasting.
- If two notes share a recipe but not a warehouse, they are not the same drink.
