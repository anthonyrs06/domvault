# Rank Is a Lagging Indicator

> The org chart is last week's grooming graph. Rank is a photograph of who sat with whom, printed after the edges already moved.

## The connection
In a macaque troop, titles catch up later. Trust routes along current edges: who grooms whom this morning, not who held the top perch last season. An org chart is a lagging indicator of that graph. A second brain makes the same mistake when it treats official folders as the map. The wiki-link graph is where load actually routes. Folders are last week's alliances, frozen into a tree.

## Why it matters
Most products instrument rank (titles, ticket queues, folder trees) and miss the live edges. By the time a title updates, the grooming has already moved. Publishing into a constellation by folder is shipping the org chart. Publishing by wiki-link is shipping the current graph.

## So what
- Trust the live edges over the printed rank.
- When a folder and a wiki-link disagree, believe the link.
- Treat a new title as a lagging report, not as a routing change.
