# The Rickhouse Is a Neighborhood

> Barrels age by shared climate, not in a closet. Notes age by sitting next to other notes. A lone cask is not a rickhouse.

## The connection
A rickhouse is a neighborhood with weather. Barrels share heat, humidity, leaks in the roof. The same mash in two warehouses is two drinks because the neighbors were different. Aging a note in a private folder with no wiki-links is a cask in a closet. Time passed. Climate did not. A second brain that wants proof needs a rickhouse: other notes close enough to mark the liquid. Synthesis is what the warehouse does to a barrel that sat in a row.

## Why it matters
People confuse duration with aging. A note that sat alone for a year is still n=1 with a birthday. The wood is the graph. Neighbors are the climate. Publishing a planet that never sat beside another planet is pouring closet whiskey and calling it a rickhouse. The public universe should taste warehouse weather, not a timer.

## So what

- Treat a wiki-link as shared weather, not as decoration on a solitary cask.
- If a claim never sat in a row, it is not aged. It is stored. Move it into the rickhouse.
