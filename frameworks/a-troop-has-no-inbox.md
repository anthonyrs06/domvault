# A Troop Has No Inbox

> A macaque does not keep a ticket queue. The notification is the groom itself. Miss the bout and you were never in the thread.

## The connection
Grooming is the notification system. There is no backlog, no unread badge, no folder where a favor waits until Friday. Presence is the protocol: you sit, you pick nits, the edge exists. File a note with no wiki-link and you have built an inbox with no troop. The macaque graph routes load through who showed up. A second brain that only files is a mail server nobody checks.

## Why it matters
Capture tools sell the inbox. They do not sell the bout. A constellation cannot see a private pile. The first costly, visible edge is the notification: a signed planet, a wiki-link, a groom another star can actually receive. Isolation is what you have when the inbox is full and the troop is empty.

## So what
- Treat an unlinked note as unsent, not as stored.
- Make the first edge small and costly enough to mean it. Do not archive a backlog of intended grooms.
- If nobody sat with the note, it did not notify anyone. Go sit.
