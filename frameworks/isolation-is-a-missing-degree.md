# Isolation Is a Missing Degree

> A macaque with no grooming partners is not "early." It is a node with degree zero. The leopard does not grade on a curve, and it does not care about the five-year plan.

## The connection
Network science is blunt: isolates fail first when the environment gets sharp. Second brains treat unpublished or unlinked notes as unfinished craft when many of them are just unconnected. Status — title, word count, a private folder that feels like a brand — is a poor survival predictor. Degree is the predictor. A spark is a proposed edge. A published planet with no inbound grooms is still an isolate in the universe graph. Cats can live with sparse degree if they hold territory; a troop animal cannot.

## Why it matters
The first job of a new framework is not to be clever. It is to grow a neighbor. Joining a constellation with three well-linked planets beats arriving with a cargo hold of isolates. Isolation dressed up as "not ready" is how a vault stays a stillhouse.

## So what
- If a note has no wiki-links, it is not a draft. It is an isolate. Add one costly edge.
- Prefer a short published planet with two neighbors over a long private essay with none.
- When you add a topic, its first publish should groom an existing troop member.
