# A Shared Sill Is a Treaty

> Two cats on one windowsill is negotiated occupancy, not friendship. The peace is a treaty about time and space, not a declaration that they like each other.

## The connection
Felids share a house by corridor, not by pack. A sill at noon, a hallway at six, a shelf that is not a throne. Overlap is not merger. Overlapping notes need the same deal: a time-share treaty, not a forced collapse into one file. A synthesis corridor is the treaty. A merge that crowns a winner is a stare.

## Why it matters
Knowledge graphs fail when they treat overlap as duplication to delete. Two notes on one sill can both be true at different hours. Publishing a planet is claiming a patch another star can occupy without a fight. If two ideas scrape fur, write the treaty. Do not crown a boss and call it hygiene.

## So what
- When two notes overlap, write a time-share, not a merge.
