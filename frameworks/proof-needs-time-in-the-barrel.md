# Proof Needs Time in the Barrel

> You can raise the proof overnight. You cannot fake the years in wood. The barrel is a slow filter: it subtracts the harsh, keeps the signal, and writes a record into the liquid that a still cannot.

## The connection
Whiskey is a control loop with a long sample interval. Distillation is the high-gain first pass — you get alcohol, you also get heat and noise. The barrel is regularization: oxygen leaks in, water and fusel oils move out, lignin and vanillin dissolve, and the same mash becomes something you would actually serve. Shipping a first idea is distillation. Letting it sit against other notes — monkeys, airplanes, a second brain — is the barrel.

## Why it matters
A constellation that only publishes hot distillate tastes like a pitch deck. The public universe should get the aged pour: genericized, linked, signed. That is why Domvault's default is `publish: false` and why the diff comes first. Time in the barrel is the consent gate wearing a tasting-room metaphor.

## So what
- Distill fast (inbox). Age on purpose (frameworks). Publish only what survived the wood.
- Score a note by what the barrel subtracted — names, clients, heat — not by how strong the first pour was.
- If two topics share a finish (trust, patience, trim), put a wiki-link. That is the angel's share becoming an edge.
