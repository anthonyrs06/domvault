A second brain is two things wearing one coat: the content — your actual notes — and the engine that connects them. This book is what the engine surfaced. Fifty-one frameworks and one thesis, genericized from a single working vault, all circling the same idea: intelligence and memory are both the art of packing more meaning into denser, retrievable representations. Learning is compression. The same kernel shows up in language models and leadership, in backtests and 1:1s, in Goodhart's law and go-to-market. Each chapter is short: the connection, why it matters, and what to do about it.
